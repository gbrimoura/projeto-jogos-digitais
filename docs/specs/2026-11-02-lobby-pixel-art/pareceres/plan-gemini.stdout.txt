### RISKS

1. **Draw Layering Occlusion:** If `lobby_visual.gd` draws the backdrop after the sign/indicator calls, dynamic UI elements could be hidden. Layer 0 draw sequence must explicitly place backdrop texture first, followed by amber plaque, bitmap text, and indicator rects.
2. **Sub-pixel Shifting / Edge Artifacts:** Non-integer camera positions or fractional offsets could induce jitter with nearest sampling; all destination rects in `_draw()` must remain strictly cast to integer world coordinates.
3. **Palette Bleed in Aseprite Scripting:** Procedural Lua generation could inadvertently produce color ramps or semi-transparent anti-aliased edge pixels if blend modes or opacity filters are applied without strict thresholding.
